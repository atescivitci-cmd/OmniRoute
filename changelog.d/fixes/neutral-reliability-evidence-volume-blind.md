- **fix(routing):** fit-first ranking no longer rewards usage volume or the largest published spec. Operational reliability is the observed 24 h success rate (`1 - max(failureRate, errorRate)`) instead of a Beta(2,2) posterior mean, and the health factor follows the observed rate continuously (no 0.5 cliff, no 10/20-sample gates). Below 3 usage samples a thin sample may only lower a score (`min(0.5, rate)`), so a failing model never looks better than neutral and a healthy one is not promoted by one lucky call (accepted 0.06 cold-start premium at critical). Hard-veto and provenance semantics are unchanged; `errorRate` for the legacy scorers keeps its 10-sample gate and fit-first reads a new `usageFailureRate`.
- **fix(routing):** capability credit is demand-relative. Task fitness and complexity fit measure a model's verified context window, output cap, and (when demanded) reasoning and tools against what the request needs (prompt size with a 0.6 context headroom, requested output or an 8192-token default, reasoning and tools on the wire), so every model that covers the request ties on fit instead of the one with the larger spec winning. A shortfall lowers the score continuously: a requested output above a model's cap is penalised in proportion to the shortfall instead of a flat -80 step, and context credit shrinks smoothly past 60% of the window while the hard -200 overflow rule stays at 85%. The flat soft penalty for unverified reasoning on heavy/critical asks is removed (reasoning the request does not demand neither rewards nor penalises); required reasoning keeps its -120 gate. The legacy 12-factor scorer and the combo inspector use a fixed neutral demand.
