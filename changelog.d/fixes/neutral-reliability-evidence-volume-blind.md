- **fix(routing):** fit-first ranking no longer rewards usage volume. Operational reliability is the observed 24 h success rate (`1 - max(failureRate, errorRate)`) instead of a Beta(2,2) posterior mean that scored an all-success model 0.60 at n=1 and 0.94 at n=29, and the health factor follows the observed rate at any sample size instead of requiring 10 samples to earn it and 20 to be called failing. Two models with the same observed success rate now tie on both terms and the neutral per-conversation tie-break decides; failures still count in full (monotone), unobserved models keep the neutral 0.5 prior, and hard-veto and provenance semantics are unchanged. Sample count and confidence stay in `routing_decisions.factors_json` as informational fields only.
